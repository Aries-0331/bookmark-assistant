# Code of Conduct

This project follows a practical standard for respectful collaboration.

## Expected Behavior

- Be direct and constructive.
- Assume good intent, but make technical claims testable.
- Keep discussion focused on the work.
- Respect different experience levels and backgrounds.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeated off-topic or disruptive comments.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove comments; close issues; or restrict participation when behavior harms the project.
